How I work

I'm a backend developer focused on stable, maintainable, scalable systems, most of them large monolithic applications. Good code, to me, should be readable enough to explain itself. In practice that means automated tests, static analysis, deliberate design patterns, and Clean Architecture principles as defaults, not afterthoughts.

I genuinely enjoy programming. This keeps me proactive about improving my own skills and treating every project, big or small, with the same amount of care.

Currently, I am expanding my engineering toolkit by mastering Python & FastAPI through personal projects, focusing on building asynchronous APIs and leveraging LLM capabilities to enhance language learning process.
